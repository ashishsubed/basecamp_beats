The night

Walk show

A Halloween walk show to open the night — costumes on the floor, lights low, and the whole room watching the looks go by.

Costume competition

We pick 10–15 finalists from the walk show. The room votes live — $100 and a trophy to the winner. Come dressed. Make it count.

Open floor for DJ

After the show, the floor stays open — remixes, party anthems, and Nepali energy until 1 AM.

Music, food & drinks

A full night of good vibes: music, food, and drinks in one room. The same community energy — with a Halloween twist.
